Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account and use Spendly ("the Service"), we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password, along with the expense records, categories, amounts, dates, and descriptions you choose to log.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate the Service — to display your expenses, generate summaries and category breakdowns, and let you filter your spending by date range. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and are retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your records will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal expense data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to run the Service, and these providers only process data as necessary to keep Spendly running.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on the Service. We're happy to help.
